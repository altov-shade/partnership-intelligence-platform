# Geef | Partnership Intelligence

**AI-Powered Sponsorship Research & Strategic Decision Support**

## Overview

Geef is a partnership intelligence application designed to support sponsorship research, opportunity evaluation, and strategic business development.

The project explores how artificial intelligence can help transform information into actionable insights for sports, entertainment, and brand partnerships.

## The Business Challenge

Identifying valuable partnerships requires understanding potential partners, business objectives, audience alignment, and strategic opportunities.

Traditional research can be time-consuming and fragmented across multiple sources.

## The Solution

Geef explores an AI-assisted approach to organizing partnership research
